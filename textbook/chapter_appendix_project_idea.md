# EduVision 🎨✨
### *Where Kids' Drawings Come to Life*

---

## What is EduVision?

EduVision is a classroom tool that turns children's hand-drawn sketches into animated characters — and then uses those characters to tell a story together as a class.

A child draws a farmer on paper. Another draws a dog. Another draws a cow.
The teacher uploads all the drawings and writes a short story:
> *"The farmer walks to the barn. The dog jumps around. The cow stands and watches."*

EduVision reads the story, understands what each character should do, and **brings every drawing to life** — the farmer walks, the dog jumps, the cow stands idle. All of them animated together in one shared scene.

**The class watches their own drawings move. On screen. As a story.**

---

## Why Does This Matter?

Most educational tools make kids *consumers* — they watch something someone else made.

EduVision makes every child a *creator*. Their drawing, no matter how simple or silly, becomes part of the story. Every child contributes. No one is left out.

It connects:
- **Creativity** — kids draw freely, no rules
- **Storytelling** — the teacher shapes a narrative
- **Technology** — real AI brings it to life

And it works for kids who can't write yet, can't type yet, or struggle with standard classroom participation. All they need to do is draw.

---

## How It Works (Simple Version)

```
1. Teacher writes a story topic
        ↓
2. Kids draw their characters on paper
        ↓
3. Teacher uploads all the sketches
        ↓
4. AI figures out what each character should do in the story
        ↓
5. The drawings are animated automatically
        ↓
6. Everyone watches the story play out — made entirely from their drawings
```

No coding. No animation skills. No expensive software. Just draw and upload.

---

## Who Is It For?

**Primary users:** Children in pre-school and early primary (ages 4–8)  
**The person who operates it:** The class teacher  
**The audience:** The whole class, and parents who can be sent the final animation

## What We're Building Next

- [ ] More motion options — run, sit, clap, idle, celebrate
- [ ] AI story reader — teacher types the story, AI assigns the right motion to each character automatically
- [ ] Web interface — upload sketches, type the story, watch the result, all in one page
- [ ] Multi-character scene — all drawings animated together in one video, not separate files
- [ ] Download & share — teacher can download the animation and share it with parents

---

## Ideas We Want to Add Later

- [ ] Voice narration — the story is read aloud over the animation
- [ ] Background scenes — choose a background (farm, classroom, jungle) for the story
- [ ] Non-human characters — animate animals, vehicles, trees, not just people
- [ ] Student gallery — every child's character is saved so parents can see it

## The Team

This is a final year project. The team is split across two tracks:

**Track A (AI Model Training)** — Training a custom AI model that can specifically recognize characters in children's sketches (handed to teammates)

**Track B (Product)** — Building the full web product: the animation engine, the story AI, and the interface (main development focus)
